About NAGARAM

NAGARAM is a unified intelligent platform connecting citizens, farmers, experts, NGOs, volunteers and government authorities to report, track and resolve civic and agricultural issues through coordinated, data-driven action.

Our Mission

To create a transparent, efficient bridge between the people who identify problems and the people who can solve them — eliminating delays, improving accountability, and building stronger communities.

Technology

Built with Python/Flask, Supabase PostgreSQL, and modern web standards. Designed for accessibility, low-connectivity environments, and multi-language support.
