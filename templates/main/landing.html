How NAGARAM Operates

A transparent 4-stage pipeline from report submission to verified resolution

1. Report & Capture

Citizens and farmers log issues with location tag, category, description, and upload photo evidence.

2. Categorize & Route

Intelligent routing automatically assigns issues to appropriate authorities, agricultural experts, or registered NGOs.

3. Coordinate & Act

Responders post progress updates, diagnose problems, and dispatch resources to the field.

4. Resolve & Verify

Resolution evidence is submitted, reporters are notified, and administrative verification closes the ticket.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NAGARAM unified platform

How does NAGARAM connect civic reports to action takers? +
NAGARAM uses role-based automated triage to match reported civic problems with municipal authorities, local NGOs, and verified volunteers based on location and category.
How do farmers get pest and crop disease diagnoses? +
Inside the Farmer Portal, farmers upload crop leaf or plant images under Crop Health Monitor. Qualified agronomy experts review the submission, provide specific pathology diagnoses, and suggest dosage and treatments.
Can NGOs and Volunteers take action on unassigned issues? +
Yes! Registered NGOs and volunteers can browse community issues needing help in their district, self-assign tasks, log progress, and record resolved impact.
